EMBERLINE — a round-based tactical FPS in the browser on three.js: Ember attacks, Slate defends. Plant the bomb at Site A (the warehouse) or Site B (the tank farm), or eliminate the defence. Earn money each round, buy rifles, SMGs, a sniper and grenades between rounds, and win 5 rounds to take the match at Kestrel Yard. Single-player against seven bots that hold lanes, push sites, plant, defuse, and shoot back in bursts.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Click DEPLOY to lock the mouse: WASD to move, mouse to aim, click to fire, right-click to aim down sights, R to reload, C to crouch, Shift to walk quiet, G for a grenade, B to buy during the buy phase, E to plant the bomb on a site.
